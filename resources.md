## History and Pedagogy of Mathematics

* [Euclid's Elements](https://aleph0.clarku.edu/~djoyce/java/elements/elements.html): the complete Elements online, with commentary by David E. Joyce (Clark University).
* [Cut the Knot](https://www.cut-the-knot.org/): Alexander Bogomolny's interactive mathematics miscellany and puzzles.
* [Math History (ancient to modern)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLIljB45xT85DfokRNdx5eQ_qA43BwyM-_): video lectures on the history of mathematics by N. J. Wildberger.
